# FEATURE-BF15-PHASE02 — Grid-drawn small icons

## Summary

The six small drawings of `final3/` — git-client, markdown-editor, text-editor, hardcopy, backup,
licensing — are redrawn on the live area 16..240 instead of scaled: each keeps its `final2` design, its
longest side spans the live area, it is centred, and every horizontal and vertical edge sits on the
16-unit grid, so the 16 and 32 px frames have whole-pixel edges. They are tile-free from the start (the QR
finder holes and the licence seal's gap are cut-outs). `final3/` now has exactly `final2/`'s file set, and
the icon no longer changes size between the 32 px (small) and 40 px (master) frames.

## Deviations & follow-ups

- **`LIVE` drives the masters only.** The small drawings are laid out by hand on the 16..240 grid (a
  computed layout could not keep edges on whole pixels); `LIVE`'s comment says they must be redrawn if it
  changes.
- **git-client is 2 units left of centre** (bounds 48..204): the branch node stays smaller than the main
  nodes, as in every git-client drawing, and both lines stay on the grid. Margins still differ by ≤ 1 px.
- **Renderer:** as PHASE01 — headless Edge stood in for `rsvg-convert` (see that record).
- **Legibility at 16 px** (6× zoom checked on light, dark and checkerboard backgrounds):
  - licensing: the ribbon tails shrink to a few faint pixels under the seal (as in `final2`); the seal and
    its gap still read.
  - markdown-editor: the arrow is a 1 px shaft and a 3 px head; it reads as "M↓" but is the weakest mark.
  - msi, launchpad, file-explorer have no small drawing, so their 16–32 px frames come from the scaled
    master with soft, half-covered edges (unchanged from `final2`). Follow-up: grid-drawn small drawings
    for these three would sharpen them.
  - text-editor (1 px cursor), backup (2×2 px keyhole), hardcopy, git-client: crisp and legible.

## Decisions taken at build time

| Question | Chosen | Why | Alternatives rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| git-client weight | 32-unit lines, main nodes r 32, branch node r 28 | 25.6 % at 24 px; branch node keeps its smaller size | All nodes r 30 (exactly centred, loses the size difference) |
| markdown-editor frame proportions | 224 × 128 (1.75 : 1), outer radius 16 | Nearest the small drawing's 1.625 : 1 on the grid; radius closer to the master's | 224 × 160 (1.4 : 1, a squarer, different-looking frame) |
| text-editor window | 224 × 192, gutter 2 units, status bar 3 units, cursor 1 × 3 units | Keeps the window's proportions and every edge on the grid | 224 × 224 (square window) |
| hardcopy finder | 6 units: ring 1, hole 1, centre 2; accent block 4 units centred in its quadrant | QR finder proportions on the grid | Scaling the 5-unit finders (half-pixel edges) |
